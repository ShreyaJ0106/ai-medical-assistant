FEVER
Common causes:
- Viral fever
- Influenza
- Dengue
- Malaria
- Typhoid

Symptoms:
- High temperature
- Chills
- Sweating
- Headache

Advice:
Drink plenty of fluids.
Take adequate rest.
Consult a doctor if fever lasts more than 3 days.

-------------------------------------------------

COMMON COLD

Symptoms:
- Sneezing
- Runny nose
- Sore throat
- Mild cough

Advice:
Stay hydrated.
Take sufficient rest.
Use steam inhalation if required.

-------------------------------------------------

MIGRAINE

Symptoms:
- Severe headache
- Nausea
- Sensitivity to light

Advice:
Rest in a dark room.
Avoid loud sounds.
Consult a neurologist if frequent.

-------------------------------------------------

GASTROENTERITIS

Symptoms:
- Vomiting
- Diarrhoea
- Stomach pain

Advice:
Drink ORS.
Stay hydrated.
Seek medical attention if symptoms worsen.

-------------------------------------------------

HYPERTENSION

Symptoms:
Usually no symptoms.

Possible signs:
- Headache
- Dizziness

Advice:
Monitor blood pressure regularly.
Consult a physician.
